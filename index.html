Educational pricing demonstration by Sundarram Datla. Assumes geometric Brownian motion, constant volatility and interest rate, no dividends, and European exercise. CRR is evaluated only when 0 ≤ p ≤ 1. All calculations and charts are generated locally with JavaScript and SVG; no third-party chart library is required.
